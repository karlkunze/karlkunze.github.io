Greetings! My name is Karl Kunze, and I am a Hot Pepper Breeder at HM Clause USA based in Davis, CA. I work on increasing efficiency of the hot pepper breeding program and integrating genomic approaches to selection for hot pepper. In addition to my profession as a plant breeder, some of my personal activities/interests include bird watching, hiking/nature in general, history, cooking, gardening, video games and snowboarding. I hope to get back into video games soon, teach myself some new artistic skills, and use my _mild_ experience in coding to help with community projects.

This GitHub site is a go-to for personal coding projects and as a host of my academic work from my PhD and post-doc. I also have a [personal website](https://karlkunze.io) that is a perpetual work in progress.
